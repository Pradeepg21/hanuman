# Privacy Policy: Hanuman Run

**Effective date:** 24 November 2026
**App:** Hanuman Run: Leap to Lanka (Android, package `com.satya.hanuman`)

Hanuman Run is an offline game. This policy explains what happens to your information when you play it.

## Information we collect
**None.** The app does not collect, store on a server, share or sell any personal or device information.

- The app has no internet permission and cannot send data anywhere.
- No account or sign-in is needed.
- There are no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools.
- There are no in-app purchases.

## Data stored on your device
Your game progress (best score, coins, gems, shop upgrades, missions and settings such as sound, music and vibration) is saved only on your phone, inside the app's private storage. It never leaves the device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data deletes it permanently.

## Permissions
- **Vibrate:** used only for short vibrations during gameplay. You can turn it off in the game's Settings.

## Children
The game is suitable for a general audience and does not knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
